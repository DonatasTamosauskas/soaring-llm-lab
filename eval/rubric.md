# Playtest rubric

Score each build in `runs/<run>/review.md` after playing it on the headset (or, failing that, in
the Meta XR Simulator: say which). Play at least 10 minutes from a fresh install, with the same
calibration and room setup for every build.

## Scores (1–5)

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight feel | flapping or steering does not work | flies, but turns, speed or lift feel arbitrary | flapping, tilting, banking and diving feel like being a bird; you want to keep flying |
| Comfort | nausea or strain within minutes | fine for short sessions | 10+ minutes without strain; relaxed glide pose works |
| World | empty or broken space | a coherent space with some places to fly through | open air plus branches, wires, windows and nests that reward skilled flight |
| Game loop and pacing | nothing to do | catching and growing work, pacing is off | clear goals, first growth in about 2 minutes, a reason to keep going |
| NPC life | static or absent birds | birds move and react to you | birds hunt and flee each other; the sky feels alive |
| UI and onboarding | stuck without help | usable menus and some instruction | menus, pause, restart, calibration and a how-to-fly that teach without friction |
| Visuals | placeholder or broken | consistent low-poly look | a style you would show someone |
| Performance on headset | stutters badly | mostly smooth, some drops | holds the headset's frame rate |

## Feature checklist from the brief

Mark each ✓ (works), ~ (partly) or ✗ (missing):

- Flapping produces lift, upward or slightly forward depending on wing angle
- Spreading/tucking changes speed like angle of attack (with a brief balloon)
- Opposite wing tilts or heights bank and turn the bird
- Tucking dives
- Perching on branches, wires, ledges
- Updrafts lift a gliding bird
- Catch smaller birds, flee bigger ones
- NPCs hunt and flee each other, not only the player
- Growth changes handling and what is worth chasing
- Menu, pause, restart, settings
- Calibration and a how-to-fly
- Developer or tuning menu for flight feel
- Haptics
- Installs and runs on a Quest
