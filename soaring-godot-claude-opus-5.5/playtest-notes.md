# Build #1 (27th 5AM)
The issues to be addressed:
- The turning mechanic is too sensitive. Small hand movements lead to unintentional turns. There should be a dead zone around the neutral position that leads to no movements. And the speed of the movement should scale non-linearly - the further out the larger the turn/other movement. Maybe this could even be a slider? (it might already be)
- Keeping the arms spread out becomes tiresome too quickly. Let's change the default 'wings out' position from a person spreading arms out wide (T pose) to a one where the person keeps its arms out to sides from the elbows, however from shoulders down to elbows the hands are going down. This is really important for the 'gliding' position, we can utilize the full arms streched out position for momentary actions.
- Somehow it is very difficult to achieve high velocity. NPC birds appear to be flying much faster and to be making much sharper turns. Why is the player character so much slower? Let's make it easier to achieve high velocity, high rates of acceleration and let's make it easy to gain altitude - large flaps of wings (maybe here we can utilize the fully extended arms) should provide major lift in elevantion / speed. This is in order to increase the fun aspect of it.
- Currently it seems that the turning mechanic of tilting the controllers works in the opposite way to what I would expect. Please review if it's set up as expected. Overall, you may want to add a developer menu in the game where I can test different movevment configurations and report back to you. I'd like an option where this movement mode can be turned off.
